Lets Work Code of Conduct

Lets Work is a company that provides co-working space to businesses, individuals, professionals, firms etc. who are from diverse backgrounds. In order for everyone to coexist cordially we have a set of rules and regulations to be followed by every person acting as an individual or representing its company/business. Following are the best practices we expect to be adhered to by everyone:

1) Don’t Angry me: To maintain the floor decorum, everyone is required to attend their meetings/calls or any conversation between individuals at a volume which would not disturb co-workers’ work.

2) Own your mess: Before leaving, check your desk — if it has any leftovers of food or stationery waste, please clean it up yourself as good as you want it to be when you come the next day.

3) Utha Le Re Baba: We offer mesmerising views and comfortable seating, which can tempt everyone to keep sipping coffee and snacks — please do not overuse any of the services/amenities and let’s make use of all resources fairly.

4) Pehchan Kaun: We have a lot of really cool people working out of the space. A friendly attitude helps everyone network better — go and get to know your next desk neighbour.

5) Paw Paw: While furry companions are a treat to play with, we are not yet equipped to provide for their comfort, hence no pets are allowed.

6) Bahut ghoor raha hai: Intimidating, harassing, abusive, discriminatory, derogatory, or demeaning speech or actions by anyone in our space, online networks, and in all related events and communication will not be tolerated. Harassment includes, but is not limited to, offensive verbal comments related to gender, gender identity and expressed sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity or religion; sexual images in public spaces; deliberate intimidation; stalking; following; harassing photography or recording; sustained disruption of talks or other events; inappropriate physical contact; and unwelcome sexual attention.

7) Control Uday Control!!!: Prolonging use of conference rooms and meeting rooms beyond the allotted time causes delays for everyone else who needs that space. Be mindful that meetings do not hamper other coworkers’ schedules — time management is crucial when booking a meeting room in a shared workspace.

8) Parampara. Pratishtha. Anushasan: We urge you to respect the privacy of your co-workers and never tamper with their belongings. Each business is unique, and so is the information resulting from it — each co-worker is required to keep all information and/or data strictly confidential, whether related to their own business or others’.

9) Swagat Nahi Karoge Aap Hamara: We welcome you to our space only if you are here to work for a legal purpose. We strictly oppose any illegal activities in the premises and online through the use of our internet, including but not limited to consuming drugs or encouraging the use or sale of drugs, cybercrimes, online frauds, and visiting unlawful websites. We reserve the right to take necessary legal action against the said person.

10) Pehnava: You are expected to report to work in neat and presentable attire. Our objective in establishing a dress code is to enable everyone to project a professional, business-like image while enjoying the advantage of more casual and relaxed clothing. Business casual and semi-formal wear is the standard. Casual dressing is allowed every Saturday but shorts are restricted for both men and women.
